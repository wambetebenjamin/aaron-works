## Notes on people and representation

Every human subject shown is Black / East African or of African descent, chosen
to represent the marketplace's Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Kampala and Dar es
Salaam audiences. Pexels descriptions sometimes label subjects "African
American" because stock libraries do not record nationality; the selected frames
are the ones whose subjects and settings best match an East African context
(several are explicitly shot in Nairobi or Kiambu, as noted above).

Portraits are illustrative placeholders for a demo marketplace and do not depict
real Aaron Works freelancers. Names, reviews and work histories are fictional.

## Not used

Candidates rejected during selection remain in the untracked `image-search/`
working folder (Getty/iStock, Shutterstock, Vecteezy, mockuphunt and unsuitable
subjects). They are **not** shipped in `public/`.
